I'm always up for a challenge and ready to learn something new.

I'm passionate about designing beautiful, well-cultivated spaces, and I believe my work will always have an underlying goal of bringing the outside in because of the healthy energy that nature brings to a space.

I'm finishing a Bachelor of Science in Interior Design with a minor in Business Administration at Meredith College in Raleigh. Internships at PMC Commercial Interiors and with Megan Molten gave me hands-on experience in both commercial and residential design, from bid documents and CAD drawings to client meetings and site visits. I also studied abroad in Italy in spring 2024.

I grew up in Fayetteville, where my family still lives. Outside the studio I love reading, yoga, and traveling both at home and abroad.
